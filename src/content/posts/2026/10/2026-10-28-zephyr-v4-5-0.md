We are pleased to announce the release of Zephyr version 4.5.0.

For a closer look at some of the key additions in this release, check out the [announcement article](https://www.zephyrproject.org/zephyr-4-5-release-blog/) on the Zephyr blog, along with its [companion video](http://zephyrproject.org/zephyr-4.5-video).

Major enhancements with this release include:

## New architectures: Infineon TriCore and Qualcomm Hexagon
Zephyr now supports the [Infineon TriCore architecture](https://docs.zephyrproject.org/latest/boards/index.html#arch=tricore), used in AURIX automotive microcontrollers, and the [Qualcomm Hexagon DSP architecture](https://docs.zephyrproject.org/latest/boards/index.html#arch=hexagon).

## Multimedia Pipeline (mpipe)
The new experimental [Multimedia Pipeline (mpipe)](https://docs.zephyrproject.org/latest/services/mpipe/index.html#mpipe) subsystem, inspired by GStreamer, provides a framework for building media applications from source, transform and sink elements linked into a zero-copy pipeline.

## Networking enhancements
Optimizations across the IP stack deliver up to 21% higher TCP throughput in the [zperf loopback benchmark](https://docs.zephyrproject.org/latest/samples/net/zperf/README-loopback-icount.html#zperf-loopback-icount).
