Electronica India 2026, held Sept. 16-18 in Bengaluru, showed how utilities and device makers can build smart meters that work across fixed networks, walk-by collections and multiple wireless protocols. At booth H3.G11, Semtech showcased how LoRa® technology now extends from low-power sensing to network discovery and&nbsp;multi-protocol connectivity across LoRaWAN®, wireless M-Bus (wM-Bus), Wireless Smart Utility Network (Wi-SUN), and mesh networking, supported by India's homegrown LoRa ecosystem.
