This paper investigates total energy shaping of underactuated mechanical systems with gravity compensation evaluated at the desired equilibrium instead of throughout the online closed-loop operation. Within the interconnection and damping assignment passivity-based control (IDA-PBC) framework, sufficient conditions for local asymptotic stability are established in terms of integrability of a vector field and positive definiteness of an augmented potential energy. The approach is also combined with a reformulation of potential energy matching that replaces a nonhomogeneous partial differential equation with a homogeneous one and introduces an additional design term. Analytic constructions for the TORA system and a class of two-degree-of-freedom systems illustrate how the matching and integrability requirements can be addressed. The proposed control law avoids online evaluation of the configuration-dependent gravity term; offline controller synthesis and verification can still require knowledge of the gravity model.
