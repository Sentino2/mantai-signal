AI clusters are packing more compute into every rack, and every link between those racks adds to your power, cost and cable bulk. If you design or run a data center, you need short-reach interconnects that go farther than passive copper without the power draw of DSP-based cables. 
Active copper cables (ACCs) with linear equalizers are built for those links. But without shared specifications, mixing cable and equipment vendors adds integration risk. That is why 13 companies launched the Active Copper Cable Multi-Source Agreement (ACC-MSA) on Feb. 23, 2026. Semtech co-chairs the group with MACOM
